# Evidence and epistemic policy

1. **Vocabulary** — UNESCO labels, grouping and SKOS relation assert what the source vocabularies state, not a scientific definition or policy outcome.
2. **Normative/legal** — distinguish an instrument's actual text from interpretation, legal force, jurisdiction and relevant dates.
3. **Descriptive** — attribute policies and institutions to primary sources; identify implementation vs announcement.
4. **Statistical** — specify indicator definitions, denominator, territory, period, vintage and missingness.
5. **Causal** — do not infer effects from correlation. Identify designs, assumptions and counterevidence.
6. **Contested claims** — preserve conflicting evidence and viewpoints with attribution; no silent reconciliation.
7. **Wikidata and AI** — candidate leads only, not accepted canonical assertions.
8. **Promotion** — a human/domain authority must record a decision and verifiable KS evidence before facts enter KA or other domain canon. `check` alone is never scientific qualification.

Suggested controlled statuses: `source_unreviewed`, `research_candidate`, `review_pending`, `accepted_with_scope`, `contested`, `rejected`, `superseded`. These are editorial values until formalized in a domain contract.
