# Intent and scope — UNESCO Encyclopedia / Educational policy

## Why this Kristal exists

Create one independently reviewable **encyclopedic knowledge Kristal** for the education-policy domain. The UNESCO microthesaurus `mt1.10` supplies the initial controlled vocabulary; it does not define the maximum extent of the encyclopedia and does not certify explanations or factual claims.

**Central question:** How are educational goals translated into public decisions, legal rights, resources, institutions and measurable outcomes, under what circumstances and with what evidence?

## Starting corpus and problem

- Working language: **English**; other languages are out of scope for this iteration.
- Source snapshot: the existing `unesco-mt1-10` Kristal from the completed Factory RUN. The November 2026 baseline contains **41 UNESCO concepts**; verify IDs and count against the selected immutable RUN, never against a hand-crafted keyword list.
- Preserve stable UNESCO IDs, URIs and SKOS broader/narrower/related links. Classify external references as cross-branch links rather than copying foreign concepts.
- Existing Wikidata/P3916 data is **unreviewed candidate evidence**, not canonical fact or approved identity.
- Start with `concept10` / **Right to education**, then sample other policy functions before covering the full branch.

## Editorial extent

This is a **policy encyclopedia**, not only a glossary. It owns evidence-qualified accounts of policy aims, institutional arrangements, policy instruments, resource choices, implementation, impact assessment, controversies and change over time. Include legal, statistical, geographic and historical detail only when it answers an education-policy question and its source scope is explicit.

Nine research facets organize discovery: rights and norms; governance; planning and reform; access and equity; financing; teachers and workforce; standards and pathways; measurement and effects; comparative/global history. Each facet is a **research lens**, never an automatic reclassification of the 41 UNESCO concepts.

## Expected outcomes

1. A controlled, source-locked branch identity with its vocabulary and cross-branch links.
2. A structured, inspectable evidence/claims model whose assertions carry source, locator, geography, time and review status.
3. Private English encyclopedic articles, beginning with `concept10`; no publication before review.
4. A replicable branch workflow for a larger federated UNESCO/world encyclopedia; the root Kristal indexes branches without copying all their evidence.

## Boundaries of this candidate

The material added by this scope exercise is an **editorial proposal**. It is not a declaration that the 41 articles exist, nor a promotion of any source data to domain KA/KS. The native model remains the original UNESCO import snapshot until the independent Authoring Kit and review process authorize an enriched v11 domain model.
